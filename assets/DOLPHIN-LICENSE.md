# Dolphin geometry

## Recommended: dolphin-smooth.glb

Adapted from **S2 File: Reconstructed symmetrical model of the dolphin**, supporting data for:

Tanaka H, Li G, Uchida Y, Nakamura M, Ikeda T, Liu H (2019). *Measurement of time-varying kinematics of a dolphin in burst accelerating swimming*. PLOS ONE 14(1): e0210860.

- Article: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210860
- Model: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210860.s005
- Original direct file: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article/file?type=supplementary&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0210860.s005
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Adaptation: centered and scaled from millimeters to meters, triangle count reduced for interactive display, smooth vertex shading; no textures or animation.
- Species: Pacific white-sided dolphin, *Lagenorhynchus obliquidens*.

Suggested compact attribution for the website:

“Dolphin geometry adapted from Tanaka et al. (2019), PLOS ONE, CC BY 4.0.” Link the article and license. A credits overlay is sufficient if easily accessible.

The `dolphin-geometry-preview.png` image is a render of this same adapted geometry and should carry the same credit.
